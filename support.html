Support

We’re here for questions about Slideshow Story while the app prepares for Apple TV.

Email

support@slideshowstory.com

Include your Apple TV model and tvOS version when reporting a problem. We aim to reply within a few business days.

Common questions

Is Slideshow Story on the App Store?

Not yet. The app is coming soon to Apple TV. This site will be updated when it becomes available to download.

Do you store my photos?

No. Photos are processed on your devices. We do not upload or keep copies of your library. See our privacy policy for details.

Do I need an account?

No. Slideshow Story is designed to work without creating an account.

What devices will it support?

Slideshow Story is planned for Apple TV. Exact hardware and tvOS requirements will be listed when the app ships.

Can I request a feature?

Yes—email support@slideshowstory.com with your idea. We read every message, even if we can’t reply to each one individually.
