Support

Questions about Slideshow Story while it’s in development for Apple TV.

Email

support@slideshowstory.com

Include your Apple TV model and tvOS version when reporting a problem. We aim to reply within a few business days.

Common questions

Is Slideshow Story on the App Store?

Not yet. The app is in development for Apple TV. This site will be updated when it’s available to download.

Do you store my photos?

No. Photos are processed on your devices. See the privacy policy.

Do I need an account?

No. The app is designed to work without creating an account.

What does the remote do?

Click to pause, swipe to skip a photo, press Menu to leave the story.

Can I request a feature?

Yes—email support@slideshowstory.com. We read every message even if we can’t reply to each one.
