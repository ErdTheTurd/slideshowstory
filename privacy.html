Privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Slideshow Story (“we,” “us”) is an Apple TV app that turns a photo album into a narrated movie night. This policy covers the app and slideshowstory.com.

Summary

  • We do not collect personal data through the app or this website.
  • Your photos are processed on your devices and are not uploaded to our servers.
  • There are no accounts, sign-ins, or user profiles.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising trackers, or third-party scripts on this site.

Photos and media

Slideshow Story reads albums you choose so it can build and play a story on your Apple TV. That work happens on your devices. We do not receive, store, or transmit copies of your photos, videos, or album metadata to Slideshow Story servers.

Accounts

The app does not require an account. We do not ask you to create a username, password, or profile.

This website

This site is static HTML and CSS. It does not set cookies for tracking, does not load third-party scripts, and does not embed analytics. Your browser may still send standard technical requests (such as IP address and user agent) to the host that serves the files; we do not use that information to identify you or build profiles.

Contact by email

If you email support@slideshowstory.com, we use your address and message only to reply. We do not add support emails to marketing lists.

Children’s privacy

Slideshow Story is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@slideshowstory.com.
