Privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Slideshow Story (“we,” “us”) is an Apple TV app that turns photo albums into narrated slideshows. This policy explains how we handle information for the app and for slideshowstory.com.

Summary

  • We do not collect personal data through the app or this website.
  • Your photos are processed on-device and are not uploaded to our servers.
  • There are no accounts, sign-ins, or user profiles.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising trackers, or third-party tracking scripts.

Photos and media

Slideshow Story reads photo albums you choose so it can build and play a slideshow on your Apple TV. That work happens on your devices. We do not receive, store, or transmit copies of your photos, videos, or album metadata to Slideshow Story servers.

Accounts

The app does not require an account. We do not ask you to create a username, password, or profile.

This website

This marketing site is static HTML and CSS. It does not set cookies for tracking, does not load third-party scripts, and does not embed analytics. Your browser may still send standard technical requests (such as IP address and user agent) to the host that serves the files; we do not use that information to identify you or build profiles.

Contact by email

If you email support@slideshowstory.com, we will use your address and message only to respond to your request. We do not add support emails to marketing lists.

Children’s privacy

Slideshow Story is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site or app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@slideshowstory.com.
