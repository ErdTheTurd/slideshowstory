How it works

Three steps to movie night

From a quiet evening on the couch to a living-room premiere of your own memories.

  1. Choose an album

    Pick a photo album from your library. Slideshow Story works with the photos already on your devices.

  2. Add chapters & narration

    Group moments into chapters. Spoken titles guide the story while soft music sets the mood.

  3. Watch on the big screen

    Sit back as pan-and-zoom motion brings stills to life—a private theater for the people you love.

Privacy

Your photos stay on your devices

Slideshow Story is built so your memories never need to leave home. Processing happens on-device. We don’t collect your photos or personal data.

Read the full privacy policy
  • On-device processing

    Slideshows are created and played locally. Your library isn’t uploaded to our servers.

  • No accounts required

    There’s nothing to sign up for. Open the app, pick an album, and begin.

  • No analytics or trackers

    This site and the app are designed without third-party tracking. We don’t sell data.

FAQ

Questions

What is Slideshow Story?

An Apple TV app that turns your photo albums into narrated movie nights—cinematic pan-and-zoom slideshows with spoken chapter titles and soft background music.

Where do my photos go?

They stay on your devices. Slideshow Story processes photos on-device and does not upload your library to our servers.

Is it available to download?

Not yet. Slideshow Story is coming soon to Apple TV and is not currently listed on the App Store.

Will I need an account?

No. The app is designed to work without creating an account or signing in.

How can I get help or ask a question?

Email us at support@slideshowstory.com. More details are on our support page.
