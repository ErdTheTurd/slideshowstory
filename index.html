Tonight’s programme

Slideshow Story

An Apple TV app that turns a photo album into a narrated movie night—slow pan-and-zoom, spoken chapter cards from each photo’s date, and a soft music bed the app generates itself.

For Apple TV · In development · Not on the App Store

3

What it does

  1. Plays an album as a film. Each photo gets a slow Ken Burns pan-and-zoom, then a soft crossfade into the next.
  2. Builds chapter cards from dates. Titles like “Saturday, June 14, 2025” appear on screen and are read aloud by the TV’s built-in voice.
  3. Adds a music bed. The app generates a quiet underscore—no playlists to manage.
  4. Stays on the Apple TV. Photos never leave your devices. No account.
  5. Uses the Siri Remote. Click to pause, swipe to skip, Menu to leave.
2

How a story plays

  1. 1

    Pick an album

    Choose Favorites, Recents, or any album from your Photos library on the Apple TV.

  2. 2

    Set the pace

    Choose seconds per photo, whether chapter titles are spoken, and whether the music bed plays.

  3. 3

    Press play

    The story starts. Click the remote to pause, swipe to skip a photo, press Menu to leave.

1

Privacy

Everything runs on the Apple TV. Photos are read from your library to build and play the story; they are not uploaded to Slideshow Story servers. There is no account and no sign-in. This site is static HTML and CSS with no trackers.

FAQ

Is it on the App Store?

Not yet. Slideshow Story is in development for Apple TV.

Do my photos leave my devices?

No. Playback and narration happen on the Apple TV. Photos are not sent to Slideshow Story.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app, pick an album, press play.

How do I get help?

Email support@slideshowstory.com or visit the support page.
